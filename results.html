06 · Interpretation

What these results do—and do not—say

They support ranking quality

Out-of-sample evidence shows the model identifies stronger contenders with meaningful consistency.

They do not prove profit

Strike rate, average odds and calibration alone do not establish a profitable betting strategy.

Windows differ

V17.2 has clean evidence only for 2024–2025 because older sectional coverage is insufficient.

Markets are informative

Higher confidence groups contained shorter-priced runners, so model strength and market obviousness overlap.

The Hoofs position

This page reports model behaviour honestly. Hoofs provides analysis and context; it does not promise outcomes, prescribe stakes or sell a guaranteed system.
