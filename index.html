More than a racecard

A sharper way to
read a race.

Hoofs turns a large field of raw racing variables into a structured view of the contest—who rates strongly, where confidence holds up, and where the race carries hidden risk.

01

Predictive runner rankings

Each declared runner is assessed through a machine-learning pipeline, producing a consistent hierarchy of contenders rather than a subjective shortlist.

02

Confidence, separated

Win, place and contender strength are treated as different questions—because a horse can be reliable without being the likeliest winner.

03

Context beside the numbers

The companion report adds race shape, evidence and runner-level interpretation to help you understand what the model sees.

04
“Good analysis should make uncertainty visible—not hide it.”

Warnings, weak races and conflicting signals remain part of the picture.

Behind the model

Built by an analyst,
not a content machine.

I built Hoofs to answer the questions a standard racecard can’t: how strong is the model’s view, what evidence is driving it, and where should we be cautious?

V2 is the result of continuous model development, historical testing and daily review across UK and Irish racing. The goal is not to make racing look certain. It is to make better-informed decisions possible.

Open project · Since early 2025

Hoofs reports have been published openly since the beginning of 2025—first through the Telegram group, and later through a public GitHub Pages archive. V2 continues that record with a stronger model and a clearer reporting framework.
